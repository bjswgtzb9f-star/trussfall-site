Bridge engineering.
No shortcuts.

A puzzle game about building bridges that genuinely have to stand up. Lay out beams, trusses, and cables, release the load, and let real structural forces hand down the verdict — a span that carries, or one that buckles, snaps, and folds into the gap.

01 / The Game

Ninety-nine hand-built levels, each one a gap that has to be crossed. Early trials give you timber and a tight budget. Later you earn steel, cable, and reinforced members — and gaps wide enough that a guess won't reach the far side.

02 / Honest Physics

Every member carries tension or compression, and every member has a point where it gives. Nothing on screen is decorative — if a span holds, it holds because the forces balanced, and you can see exactly where they didn't.

03 / One Person, No Filler

TRUSSFALL is built and maintained by a single developer. Nothing to sign up for, no feeds to scroll — just the puzzles, the materials, and a bridge that either stands or doesn't.
