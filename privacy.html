Privacy Policy

Effective: 9 May 2026
The short version TRUSSFALL doesn't collect your personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising trackers, no servers receiving your gameplay data. Everything stays on your device. Delete the app and it's all gone.

What we collect

Nothing personal. TRUSSFALL is a single-player offline game. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone, or any other personal information. There is no account creation, no login, no profile.

What's stored on your device

The app saves your progress (level scores, completed trials, in-progress bridge designs, premium-unlock status, remaining hint count) using your device's local storage. This data lives only on your iPhone or iPad. We never see it. Deleting the app deletes the data.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple via StoreKit. We receive an entitlement confirmation that you successfully purchased an item; we do not see your name, payment information, or Apple ID. Apple's privacy policy governs how Apple handles your purchase data: apple.com/legal/privacy.

What we don't do

  • No third-party advertising or ad tracking.
  • No analytics SDKs (Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or similar).
  • No social media tracking pixels.
  • No data shared with anyone — there is no data to share.

Because we do not track you across apps, you will not see Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. There is nothing to track.

Children's privacy

TRUSSFALL is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13. In-app purchases are protected by Apple's standard parental controls. If a child uses TRUSSFALL, we recommend enabling Ask to Buy on their Apple ID via Family Sharing.

Your rights (GDPR / EU)

If you live in the European Economic Area, the UK, or another jurisdiction with similar data-protection law, you have the right to access, correct, delete, and port your personal data. Because TRUSSFALL does not collect personal data, most of these rights are automatic — there is nothing about you on our end to access, correct, or delete. To remove your on-device data, simply uninstall the app.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles data — for example, if a future version adds an opt-in feature like cloud save — we will update this page, change the "Effective" date at the top, and surface the change in-app before the new version takes effect.
