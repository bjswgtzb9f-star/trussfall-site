02 / COMMON QUESTIONS

I'm stuck on a level. What do I do?
Tap the HINT button (top-right of the game screen) to get a structural suggestion. You start with 3 free hints per game. When those run out, you can unlock unlimited hints with the Lifetime Unlock, or buy a one-time Hint Pack from the in-game store.

How do I restore a previous purchase?
Open the in-game store (⬢ Premium button, top-left of the game screen) and tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom of the modal. Your Lifetime Unlock will be restored. Hint packs are consumables and cannot be restored — that's how consumable purchases work on both the App Store and Google Play, not a bug. If you bought hints on a previous device and don't see them on a new one, the hints don't carry over.

Why does my bridge keep collapsing?
A truss collapses when one of its members is overloaded. After the live load test, look for the joint that snapped first — then add more diagonal support around it, or upgrade that section to a stronger material. The AUTO ⚡ button can apply a tested template for the current level's terrain if you want a starting point to learn from.

Which material should I use?
Wood is cheap and light but weak — best for the early levels with short spans. Steel is your workhorse: strong, stiff, and balanced for most mid-game levels. Cable is tension-only (it can only pull, never push) and is used for suspension and cable-stay bridges. Reinforced concrete is the heaviest and strongest material, unlocked at level 50, for the late-game wide spans.

Does TRUSSFALL work offline?
Yes. The entire game runs on-device. The only network activity is when you purchase or restore an in-app purchase, which is handled by Apple's StoreKit on iOS, or Google Play Billing on Android.

03 / BUG REPORTS

If you encounter a crash, visual glitch, or unexpected behavior, please email hello@trussfall.com with:

  • What you were doing when it happened
  • Which level you were on (if applicable)
  • Your device model and OS version
  • A screenshot or screen recording if possible

Reproducible bugs get fixed faster — anything you can share helps.

04 / REFUND REQUESTS

iOS — refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple ID used for the purchase. Apple typically responds within 48 hours.

Android — request a refund through Google Play: open the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, find the order and choose the refund option. Purchases made within the last 48 hours are usually refunded automatically. After that window, or if Google declines, email hello@trussfall.com — on Google Play we can issue a refund ourselves.
